Profile

About

I am Yudong Wang — also known as Zake — an undergraduate in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Beijing Institute of Technology, expected to graduate in 2029.

My current research is multi-view clustering, especially constraint-based and active methods. I reproduce recent papers, audit where the released code departs from the paper, and build experiment pipelines for stability, ablation, and noisy-constraint studies. I care less about a single best ARI than about whether a gain survives different seeds, preprocessing choices, and wrong answers.

I also keep a Chinese-language technical blog, where I write up clustering notes, CS study guides, and paper reading. Earlier applied work includes pixel-level foreign-object detection on 4K industrial images.

Multi-View Clustering: Reproduction & Evaluation

Undergraduate research · 2026 · PyTorch, scikit-learn, UMAP

A private research workspace for reproducing and stress-testing recent multi-view clustering methods before building on them.

  • Ran TriGAC (active constrained multi-view clustering) on 3Sources, BBCSport, and MNIST-4, and built a paper-budget vs. observed-ARI comparison.
  • Rebuilding GLGC (CVPR 2026) from a fixed upstream commit: audited paper–code differences in feature dimensions, decoder order, loss weighting, and graph scope, then implemented a paper-aligned adapter covered by automated tests.
  • Audited and adapted Fruit, Fruit360, and Cards for Multi-MaP / Multi-Sub comparison runs under a shared evaluation protocol.
  • Next steps: seed and preprocessing stability, module ablations, and Must-Link / Cannot-Link noise experiments.

Foreign-Object Detection on 4K Tobacco Cut Surfaces

Research engineering · 2026 · Python, PyTorch, OpenCV, PyQt6

Code ↗

Pixel-level localization of paper, plastic, metal, and fiber contaminants on 4080×3060 production images — not image-level “is there an anomaly?” classification.

  • Showed why text-anchor CLIP approaches fail here: normal and abnormal prompts stay nearly parallel (cosine ≈ 0.91–0.96), and discriminative losses collapse on normal-only data.
  • Switched to unsupervised reconstruction (INP-Former), trained on 50 normal images without masks, and detected all foreign objects in 6/6 held-out images with clean backgrounds.
  • Built the reusable engineering around it: ROI extraction, globally normalized 4K sliding-window inference, ROI-weighted loss, and a four-view PyQt6 inspection GUI.
  • Documented the failed routes and engineering pitfalls under Apache-2.0 so the negative results are reusable.
